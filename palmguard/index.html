How it works

One small guardrail. No new habit.

  1. 1

    Keep tap to click on

    PalmGuard does not ask you to change how your trackpad works.

  2. 2

    Choose a brief delay

    After a key press, accidental left-click events are ignored during that short window.

  3. 3

    Work normally

    Pointer movement, scrolling, and keyboard input continue as usual.

Privacy

Your input stays on your Mac.

PalmGuard observes only the event types needed to prevent accidental clicks. It does not capture text content or build an activity history.
