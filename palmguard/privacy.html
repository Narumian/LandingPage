Privacy summary

Input stays private.

PalmGuard uses the minimum event information needed to prevent accidental taps after typing.

What PalmGuard does not collect

  • Typed text, keyboard shortcuts, clipboard data, or documents.
  • Application names, window titles, URLs, or browsing history.
  • Analytics, advertising identifiers, or a persistent input-event history.

Local settings

PalmGuard stores its enabled state, chosen suppression delay, and login-item preference locally on the Mac. Diagnostic counters exist only while the app is running.

Payments and license verification

Stripe Managed Payments processes checkout and payment details. PalmGuard does not receive or store full payment card details. The license service stores a one-way hash of each license key, an encrypted copy only until the key is claimed, the Stripe payment and session references, and license activation records. It does not store the buyer's email address with the license record.

When you activate PalmGuard, the app sends your license key, an activation identifier when validating or deactivating, and a generic device label to the PalmGuard license service on Cloudflare. The license key and activation identifier are stored in the macOS Keychain. The app does not send typed text, input history, or usage analytics.

Support messages

If you use the support form, PalmGuard stores the category and message you submit in Cloudflare D1 for product feedback. The form does not ask for your name or email address; do not include passwords, license keys, private text, or other sensitive information. Cloudflare uses your IP address to enforce a short-term submission limit, but PalmGuard does not save it with the message. Feedback is automatically deleted after 90 days. For purchase or refund requests, email cyc.sys.sys.sys@gmail.com; information you include in that email is used to respond to your request.
