If PalmGuard is not right for you, contact us within 14 calendar days of purchase for a full refund.

How to request a refund

Email cyc.sys.sys.sys@gmail.com from the address used at checkout and include your order confirmation. You do not need to provide a reason. Do not email payment card details or your full license key.

An already activated Mac may continue to work temporarily while offline, for up to the app's 30-day offline-validation grace period.

This voluntary policy does not limit any rights that apply under mandatory consumer-protection laws.
