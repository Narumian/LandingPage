Help shape PalmGuard.

Questions about PalmGuard, setup, or availability? Send a note directly from this page.

日本語でも送信できます。macOSのバージョンやMacの機種が分かると、問題を調べやすくなります。

Please do not include passwords, license keys, private text, or other sensitive information. Messages are stored for support and product improvement, then deleted within 90 days. This form does not collect your email, so individual replies are not available.

Before reporting an issue

  • Confirm that PalmGuard has Accessibility permission in System Settings when using click filtering.
  • Include the macOS version, Mac model, and the suppression delay you selected.
  • Do not include private text, screenshots of sensitive content, or license keys.
