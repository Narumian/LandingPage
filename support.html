Support

Help shape PalmGuard.

PalmGuard is currently in private beta. Feedback about accidental-tap behavior, accessibility permission, or compatibility is welcome.

Open a support request

Before reporting an issue

  • Confirm that PalmGuard has Accessibility permission in System Settings.
  • Include the macOS version, Mac model, and the suppression delay you selected.
  • Do not include private text, screenshots of sensitive content, or license keys.
